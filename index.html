Confidentiality

Nothing on this desk leaves it.

Your pipeline is the most commercially sensitive thing you own. One leak costs a deal, a client, or a reputation you spent a decade building. So this is the part we built first.

Isolation

Your agency's data sits in its own tenant. Nothing pools, nothing is queried across agencies, and no other desk can reach yours.

Access

Only the people you invite. We don't read your desk — support can't open it without your explicit, written go-ahead, per incident.

Reuse

Your deals never train a model, feed a ranking, or surface to another client. Not sold, not shared, not aggregated, not "anonymised" and passed on.

Independence

We represent no players and broker no deals. No agency, no stake in any transfer that moves through this platform, nothing to gain from what's on your desk.

Protection

Encrypted in transit and at rest, on infrastructure we monitor continuously, with access logged and reviewed.

Exit

Export everything and close your account whenever you want. Deletion means deletion — not archived, not retained "for analytics".
